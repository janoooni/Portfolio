about

Interdisciplinary social designer working across wildlife conservation, illustration, and community-based research.

I'm a designer and illustrator who works where ecology meets storytelling. Campaigns, zines, research, picturebooks, board games about poaching - if it lives at the edge of conservation and community, I'm probably interested.

Having experience in wildlife conservation design, I've led campaigns, built brand identities, shown illustration work at exhibitions across India, and facilitated public workshops. I'm currently based in New York, pursuing an MFA in Design for Social Innovation at SVA and researching wildlife trafficking and community access to nature in NYC.

Outside of work, I make a very good pesto. I'm playful by nature and will say yes to basically any game - board games, card games, ones you make up on the spot. I'm convinced the best ideas come from people who know how to not take themselves too seriously.

A note on this site

Design lately feels like a lot of pressure to shrink yourself to fit in, keep it simple, keep it minimal, keep it muted. I didn't want to do that.

The orange, green, and hot pink you see here are from home. It's what India actually looks like to me. I know how to balance minimalism when I need to, but my work has always leaned on color because it brings back a sense of heritage and belonging.

The rest of the site is just as literal. That diagram on the work page? It's genuine data visualization. Projects sit exactly where they do based on how much visual design, research, or content strategy went into them. The shape means something real. And the interactive bits - the scratching, the dots reacting when your cursor gets close - exist because I want this space to feel like play. It's the same spirit I put into everything I make.
